# SERVICES AGREEMENT — SAMPLE (FICTIONAL)

**This is a fictional sample document created for a software demonstration.
Juniper Table and Cove Supply are invented parties. No real client,
agreement, or money is involved.**

Agreement between **Juniper Table LLC** ("Client") and **Cove Supply Co.**
("Contractor"), dated October 1, 2026, for warehouse inventory services at
the Client's San Diego facility.

1. **Cycle counting.** Contractor shall cycle-count all SKUs in Warehouse B
   on or before October 15, 2026, and deliver the count report to Client
   within two business days of completion.

2. **Insurance.** Contractor shall carry general liability insurance of
   $1,000,000 and provide a certificate of insurance to Client before work
   begins.

3. **Payment.** Client shall pay Contractor a flat fee of $2,500.00 within
   net 30 days of receipt of invoice.

4. **Variance reconciliation.** Contractor shall reconcile all inventory
   variances over $500.00 with Client's finance contact before month-end
   close.

5. **Confidentiality.** Contractor shall not disclose Client's inventory
   data to any third party during the term and for 12 months after it ends.

6. **Termination.** Either party may terminate this agreement with 14 days'
   written notice to the other party.

Signed: (fictional signatures omitted)
